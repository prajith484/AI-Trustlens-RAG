Artificial Intelligence and Transformers

The Transformer architecture was introduced in 2017 in the paper "Attention Is All You Need" by Vaswani et al. at Google.

Transformers rely on the self-attention mechanism instead of recurrence, which allows efficient parallel training on large corpora.

BERT, published in 2018 by Google, is a Transformer-based encoder model for language understanding tasks.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines a retriever over trusted documents with a generator. The retriever finds evidence passages and the generator conditions on them.

Large language models such as GPT from OpenAI and models from Anthropic are often audited for factual accuracy using evidence-based verification.
