Title: Antibiotic Use

MEDICAL RAG TEST DOCUMENT — verify against the linked source before clinical use.

Antibiotics are medicines used to treat certain bacterial infections. They do not treat viral infections such as colds or flu. Using antibiotics when they are not needed can contribute to antibiotic resistance. Advice about appropriate antibiotic use should follow current guidance from a qualified healthcare professional and the source above.
